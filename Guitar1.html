PRS Custom 24 semi-hollow


This is the PRS Custom 24 semi-hollow guitar. It features a beautiful flame maple top, a comfortable mahogany neck, and PRS humbucker pickups that deliver a rich, full tone. Its flame maple top distinguishes it from other guitars and provides a unique but familiar look. It also delivers a jazzy sound due to the semi-hollow body construction. Its very popular among guitarist because of its versatility and excellent build quality.
